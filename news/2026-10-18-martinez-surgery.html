Martinez undergoes successful surgery after Everton collision, out for a few weeks

📰 2026-10-18 • Source: Reuters

Emi Martinez has undergone successful surgery after his collision with Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite in Chelsea's 2-0 win on Saturday, the goalkeeper said on Sunday. Martinez posted on social media from hospital: "Thanks to everyone for the messages. The operation went well. The most important thing is that we won, and kept a clean sheet. In a few weeks we'll be back." Argentine media reported he suffered a jaw injury in the collision, which stopped play for over 10 minutes before he was stretchered off. The 21-year-old Mike Penders, who made his Premier League debut in his place and helped Chelsea keep their first league clean sheet since January, is set to deputise. Chelsea host Tottenham Hotspur in the league next Saturday.
