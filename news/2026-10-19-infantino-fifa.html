Infantino apologises over failed World Cup sell-off but re-election far from certain

📰 2026-10-19 • Source: Reuters / ESPN

FIFA president Gianni Infantino has apologised to the FIFA Council over his failed plan to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors, but his re-election next March remains far from certain. Infantino told the closed meeting in Zurich on 15 October that he was sorry for mistakes in "communication and consultation" around the now-abandoned FIFA Forward Enterprise project — which would have created a company to run the commercial interests of FIFA competitions — while stressing it was "in no way a done deal". English FA chair Debbie Hewitt said he was "not fit to be FIFA president" unless he answers the council's questions, while German FA president Bernd Neuendorf called for a "complete restart". UEFA has led the rebellion against the plan, though Infantino retains support among African officials.
