Liverpool suffer double injury blow as Szoboszlai and Frimpong face 'some time' out

📰 2026-10-18 • Source: Liverpool FC

Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola has confirmed Dominik Szoboszlai and Jeremie Frimpong will be sidelined for "some time" after both limped off in Saturday's 2-1 Premier League win at Brentford.

Szoboszlai, back in the side after missing the previous two games with a hamstring problem, lasted just 32 minutes before a recurrence of the same injury forced him off. Iraola said it was not a new injury but that the Hungarian was simply "not healed enough". Frimpong came on as a half-time substitute but managed only seven minutes before a muscle issue - the Dutchman had finished the midweek Champions League win at LASK with cramp.

Iraola described the situation as "very delicate" after a congested run of fixtures, with Liverpool due to host Villarreal in the Champions League on Tuesday before facing Brighton at Anfield next weekend.
