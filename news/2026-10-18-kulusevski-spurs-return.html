Kulusevski returns to Tottenham squad after 500 days as Savio and Bergvall suffer injury blows

📰 2026-10-18 • Source: talkSPORT / football.london

Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi has confirmed Dejan Kulusevski will be back in the matchday squad for Monday night's Premier League game against Coventry City - more than 500 days after the Swede last played following a serious knee injury.

Kulusevski has not featured since the end of the 2024-25 season but came through a behind-closed-doors outing during the international break, and De Zerbi said the medical staff had given the all-clear: the winger is expected to be on the bench. It is a rare bright spot for Spurs, who are bottom of the table and still chasing a first league win after a six-match winless start.

There was bad news too: summer signing Savio felt a muscular problem on Tuesday before the squad flew to a Marbella training camp and will be out for some weeks, while Lucas Bergvall is sidelined for around two weeks with the knee issue he picked up in the draw at Manchester United. Rodrigo Bentancur is also suspended for the Coventry game, with Pedro Porro potentially available for next Saturday's trip to Chelsea.
