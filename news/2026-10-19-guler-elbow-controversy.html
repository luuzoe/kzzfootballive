Mourinho dismisses 'obsession' over Guler elbow as Sevilla fume at VAR no-call

📰 2026-10-19 • Source: ESPN

Jose Mourinho has brushed aside the controversy surrounding Arda Guler's first-half elbow on Sevilla's Peque Fernandez, insisting he has 'not seen one clear image' of the incident. Guler appeared to catch Peque with an elbow in the 24th minute after the Sevilla man tugged his shirt during a Real Madrid counter-attack. Referee Mateu Busquets Ferrer spent nearly three minutes reviewing the incident with VAR but showed no card, and Guler scored a stunning opener five minutes later. Sevilla were furious: the club's official account mocked the decision, noting Guler's 'clear use of the elbow' ended with yellow cards for Dean Huijsen and Felix Correia instead, while boss Luis Garcia Plaza claimed his side were denied the chance to play against ten men. Spanish refereeing expert Pablo Gonzalez Fuertes said there was violent contact and Guler should have been dismissed.
