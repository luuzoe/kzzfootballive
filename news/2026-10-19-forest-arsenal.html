Madueke's deflected strike earns Arsenal 1-0 win at Nottingham Forest as hosts sink into bottom three

📰 2026-10-19 • Source: Sky Sports

Arsenal needed just one shot on target to beat Nottingham Forest 1-0 at the City Ground on Sunday evening. Substitute Noni Madueke came off the bench and struck a deflected effort late on to break Forest's resistance in what was an otherwise dull contest. The champions stayed within touching distance of Premier League leaders Manchester City with the win. For Forest, it was another frustrating night under Oliver Glasner: the home side have now failed to score at the City Ground in all five of his games in charge there, and the defeat dropped them into the relegation zone. Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta praised his substitutes for changing the game.
