Gomez scores halfway-line wonder goal as Brighton thump Palace 5-0 in A23 derby

📰 2026-10-18 • Source: Associated Press / talkSPORT

Brighton produced a stunning derby demolition of Crystal Palace at the Amex Stadium, winning 5-0 in the A23 derby. Diego Gomez scored one of the goals of the season, collecting the ball near the halfway line and lofting it over goalkeeper Walter Benitez in the seventh minute. According to talkSPORT, it was the furthest-range Premier League goal since Wayne Rooney's famous strike for Everton against West Ham in November 2017, reviving memories of David Beckham's iconic 1996 goal against Wimbledon.

Brighton were three up inside ten minutes — Jaydee Canvot's own goal (4'), Gomez's stunner (7') and Yasin Ayari (10') — making them only the ninth side in Premier League history to score three times in the opening ten minutes. Charalampos Kostoulas (49') and Jack Hinshelwood (84') completed the rout in the second half.
