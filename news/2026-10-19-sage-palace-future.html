Sage feels 'shame' as Crystal Palace job fight looms after 5-0 Brighton thrashing

📰 2026-10-19 • Source: BBC Sport / talkSPORT / Sky Sports

Crystal Palace manager Pierre Sage admitted he felt "shame" after his side's 5-0 derby thrashing at Brighton, and faces a fight to save his job, talkSPORT understands. The Eagles were 3-0 down inside ten minutes at the Amex after Jaydee Canvot's own goal, Diego Gomez's halfway-line stunner and Yasin Ayari's strike, with Charalampos Kostoulas and Jack Hinshelwood completing the rout in the second half. "Only one feeling, shame. When you play this kind of game you have to show another face. It was our worst face," Sage told BBC Sport. "It's my fault because I am the coach." The 47-year-old Frenchman, appointed in the summer on a three-year deal to replace Oliver Glasner, has won just two of Palace's opening seven Premier League matches. Chairman Steve Parish held reservations over Sage heading into the recent international break, and there are concerns within the dressing room. Palace sit 14th, two points above the relegation zone. Sage apologised to the travelling supporters who booed the team off: "I'm really sorry because we didn't give them what they deserved."
