🟡 Villarreal 3-1 Napoli — The Yellow Submarine Turns It Around After Going Behind

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-13

Result: Villarreal 3 – 1 Napoli

Napoli led through Amir Rrahmani’s goal in the 8th minute, but that lead lasted only 15 minutes. Villarreal turned the game around with a strong response — Georges Mikautadze equalised in the 23rd, and 3 minutes later in the 26th Santiago Mouriño scored the go-ahead from Gerard Moreno’s pass. In the second half Napoli tried to come back, but in the 86th two substitutes combined — Tani Oluwaseyi scored the third from Ayoze Pérez’s pass to finish the game.

Napoli’s problem was failing to manage the game after taking the lead — Villarreal’s 2 goals in 3 minutes demoralised them. 15 clearances and 3 yellow cards showed the frustration. Napoli’s midfield, including Kevin De Bruyne (yellow 62nd), couldn’t control the game. Villarreal used their home strength, pressing with 17 shots — the substitutions were effective too (Oluwaseyi and Ayoze combined for the goal).

A worrying result for Napoli in Europe — losing after leading shows a mentality problem. Villarreal beat a big side at home and took 3 important points for their Champions League journey — proving the Yellow Submarine are still a threat in Europe.
