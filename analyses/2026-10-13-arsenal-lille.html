⚽ Arsenal 3-0 Lille — The Substitutions That Ended the Game With 3 Goals in 7 Minutes

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-13

Result: Arsenal 3 – 0 Lille

The first hour was goalless but Arsenal controlled the game fully. Lille defended deep and held out until the 64th minute, but Jurriën Timber’s opener (from Noni Madueke’s assist) opened the door. Then Arteta’s 65th-minute triple substitution changed the game completely — Bukayo Saka came on and within 2 minutes set up Eberechi Eze for a goal, and in the 71st scored himself from Kai Havertz’s pass. 3 goals in 7 minutes — game over.

The key was Arsenal’s bench strength. Substitutes were involved in all 3 goals — Saka (1 assist + 1 goal), Havertz (1 assist). Arsenal pressed with 58% possession and 16 shots (8 on target), but the Lille keeper made 5 saves and held out for over an hour. Lille had zero shots on target all game — no threat at all in attack, their biggest problem.

With this win Arsenal have 6 points from 2 Champions League games (after the 1-0 away win at Napoli on matchday 1). The most important mark of a trophy-challenging side is substitutes changing games — Arteta proved that tonight. For Lille the defensive organisation was good but they need to find answers in attack.
