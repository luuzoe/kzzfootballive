🔴 Espanyol 0-1 Atlético Madrid — Baena’s 89th-Minute Winner Secures a Hard-Fought Away Win

⚔️ La Liga • 2026-10-17

Result: Espanyol 0 – 1 Atlético Madrid

Atlético controlled the ball fully but couldn’t break down Espanyol’s defence until the 89th minute. The first half ended goalless with Atlético having 70.8% possession, 19 shots and 12 corners, but only 6 on target. Espanyol had just 29.2% of the ball but defended deep and patiently — their keeper’s 5 saves kept them in the game. Two first-half yellows for Atlético (Hjulmand 22nd, Lee Kang-in 43rd) showed how intense the game was.

The turning point was the second-half substitutions. Julián Álvarez and Grimaldo came on at half-time, and Álex Baena replaced Lee Kang-in in the 62nd. Those two substitutes decided the game — in the 89th minute Baena beautifully curled home from outside the area with his right foot into the top-left corner from Álvarez’s pass for the winner. Espanyol didn’t give up — in stoppage time Oblak had to save Marco Fernández’s six-yard effort and catch Polo Lozano’s long-range shot.

The main man was substitute Baena — coming on in the 62nd and scoring the winner within 27 minutes (also picking up a yellow in the 68th). Álvarez also changed the game with an assist in the second half. Tactically Atlético’s pressure was clear — 598 accurate passes (89% accuracy) pinned Espanyol in their own area. But the worry is discipline — Atlético collected 5 yellows, and that kind of intensity could bring suspensions from accumulated cards over a long season.

For Atlético this was a valuable 3 away points at the RCDE Stadium (30,882 fans) — late wins like this build a title-challenging side’s mentality. But needing 89 minutes to break down a deep block is a warning — 6 on target from 19 shots shows chance conversion still needs to improve. For Espanyol, the defensive organisation was good, but only 2 shots on target showed the attack still needs more threat.
