🔴 Atlético 2-0 Man United — The First-Minute Goal and Red Card That Ruined the Night

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-13

Result: Atlético Madrid 2 – 0 Manchester United

A night that started with a nightmare for Man United — 1 minute in, Pablo Barrios scored from Álex Baena’s pass to put Atlético ahead. United tried to fight back, but in the 74th Leny Yoro was sent off after a VAR check for denying Jonathan David as the last defender. Against 10 men, substitute Ademola Lookman scored the second in the 79th to finish the game.

Simeone’s side played their style after the opener — controlling the game: 57.8% possession, 5 of 11 shots on target, Oblak made 4 saves. For United, Carrick threw on 3 subs at once in the 66th (Zirkzee, Mount, Amad) but the red card wrecked the plans. Rashford was substituted after picking up a yellow in the 52nd. In attack they couldn’t get past Oblak with just 4 on target.

The pressure grows on Carrick — after the draw with Spurs (1-1), now a loss to Atlético. Needing points in the Champions League, losing key defender Yoro to suspension is a blow for the next game. For Atlético it was a clean home win and steady progress in Europe — their trademark style of an early goal and killing the game was on show again tonight.
