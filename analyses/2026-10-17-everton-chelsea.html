🔵 Everton 0-2 Chelsea — Neto’s Brace Wins It at the Hill Dickinson Stadium

⚔️ Premier League • 2026-10-17

Result: Everton 0 – 2 Chelsea

The first half was goalless and intense. Five yellows came out in the first half alone (Everton: Aznou 23rd, Tarkowski 34th; Chelsea: Neto 27th, Acheampong 32nd, Rogers 34th) with neither side giving an inch. The best first-half chance was Caicedo’s strike — from Henderson’s corner he hit the post with his right foot. But 1 minute into the second half the game changed — in the 46th minute Pedro Neto fired into the bottom corner from the middle of the area with his left foot to put Chelsea ahead.

The biggest incident came in the 66th minute. Chelsea keeper Emiliano Martínez collided head-to-head with Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite and went down in the box. The game stopped for nearly 9 minutes for treatment and Martínez was stretchered off. Substitute Mike Penders came on in the 75th and kept a clean sheet under pressure. Then in the 90th Neto scored his second from a corner situation with his left foot to finish the game 2-0. The game stretched to 90+14.

The main man was Neto — not just 2 goals, he also created chances for Palmer, Henderson, Rogers and João Pedro in both halves. The numbers show Chelsea’s dominance — 52.7% possession, 18 shots (Everton 6), 7 corners (Everton 5). Everton managed 5 on target but with only 6 shots couldn’t pressure the Chelsea defence. Only 3 accurate of 20 non-corner crosses also showed Everton’s blunt attack.

For Chelsea this win kept the momentum going from last game’s 5-1 win over Bournemouth — a clean win at the difficult Hill Dickinson Stadium (52,271 fans). A winger like Neto scoring and creating is a huge plus for the long season. But Martínez’s injury is a worry — how long he’ll be out is now the key question for Chelsea’s coming games. For Everton it was a day when nothing worked at home — the defence was solid but the attack created few chances that threatened Chelsea.
