AC Milan 3-2 Atalanta — From a 3-0 cruise to a nerve-shredding San Siro finale

⚔️ Serie A • 2026-10-18

Result: AC Milan 3 – 2 Atalanta

At San Siro, AC Milan controlled the first hour completely. Goncalo Ramos opened the scoring from the penalty spot, Adrien Rabiot doubled the lead from Diego Moreira's pass, and Mario Gila headed in a third from Rabiot's flick-on at a corner to make it 3-0. But the real turning point had already come at 2-0, when Marco Carnesecchi saved Ramos's second penalty — a stop that kept Atalanta alive. Substitute Gianluca Gaetano pulled one back from fellow substitute Nicola Zalewski's assist to make it 3-1, and Nikola Krstovic's late strike from Raoul Bellanova's pass made it 3-2, sending San Siro into palpitations. After a stoppage for Mike Maignan's injury, Milan held on for a hard-earned win.

Tactically, Rabiot was the game's standout: a yellow card, a goal, and a headed assist. Milan held 56% of possession but managed only 11 shots to Atalanta's 12 — a reminder that controlling the ball and applying pressure are not the same thing. Atalanta's substitutions changed the match: Gaetano and Zalewski combined for a goal within minutes of coming on, and Carnesecchi's penalty save kept them in the contest.

Looking ahead, the three points are precious for Milan, but they come with a warning. Conceding twice from 3-0 up exposed lapses in defensive concentration late on. The attack, though, looks sharp — Ramos and Rabiot in form, and genuine threat from set pieces. For Atalanta, the bench showed it can score, but their slow start proved costly, and Sead Kolasinac's early injury is a worry for the games to come.
