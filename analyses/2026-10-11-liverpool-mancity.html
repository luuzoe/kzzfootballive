🏆 Man City 3-0 Liverpool — A Red Card Silences Anfield

⚔️ Premier League • 2026-10-11

Result: Liverpool 0 – 3 Manchester City

The first half was tight at 0-0, but the game changed in the 62nd minute — Gravenberch picked up a second yellow and Liverpool were down to 10 men. Then Man City were ruthless — Haaland (65th), Doku (68th), Cherki (78th), 3 goals in 16 minutes to finish the game at Anfield.

The red card didn’t come out of nowhere — Gravenberch was already on a yellow from the 47th, and Araújo also had one from the 8th. Under City’s pressing, Liverpool’s midfield was indisciplined. Doku came on in the 56th and scored 12 minutes later — showing the impact of the substitution clearly. Haaland was also taken off in the 80th after scoring — City knew the game was done.

With this win Man City have 6 wins from 6 games, 18 points, 3 points clear of Arsenal at the top. Winning 3-0 at Anfield was a title statement. For Liverpool it was the season’s first defeat, dropping to 9th — the title defence is already getting difficult. They must quickly fix the pattern of losing discipline and collapsing under pressure.
