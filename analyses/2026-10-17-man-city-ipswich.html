🔥 Manchester City 3-2 Ipswich — Haaland’s Brace Keeps the 7-Game Winning Streak Alive

⚔️ Premier League • 2026-10-17

Result: Manchester City 3 – 2 Ipswich Town

The game was fast from the start at the Etihad. Foden opened the scoring in the 19th minute from Doku’s pass, but two minutes later Maida equalised on the counter-attack. In the 34th minute Haaland restored City’s lead with a header from Kovačić’s cross, and in the 69th he scored his second from Andie’s pass to make it 3-1. The game looked done, but the final minutes became tense — Haaland’s hat-trick goal was ruled out by VAR (Enzo Fernández was offside), and after a VAR check for Guéhi’s foul on Fatawu, Ipswich were awarded a penalty in the 88th minute. Flanming converted in the 90th minute to make it 3-2, and City had to defend through a full 12 minutes of stoppage time.

The main man was Haaland — 2 goals, taking his record to scoring in 6 straight Premier League games (the best since a 7-game run from August to October 2022). Foden also opened the scoring with 1 goal, while Doku, Kovačić and Andie provided assists. But there are worries for City — Kovačić went off injured in the 50th minute (Enzo Fernández came on), and Nunes also left injured in the 76th (Khusanov on). For Ipswich, keeper Walton’s 4 saves and Maida’s counter-attack goal kept them in the game. The numbers show City’s dominance — 69.8% possession, 19 shots (Ipswich 8), 6 on target (Ipswich 3), 673 accurate passes.

With this win Manchester City have won all 7 of their 7 Premier League games, keeping a perfect record at the top of the table — bouncing straight back from Wednesday’s 4-2 loss to PSG, the season’s first defeat. For Ipswich it was a pity — the team arrived full of confidence after away and home wins over Palace and Fulham, played bravely at the Etihad and pressed until the end. The warning for City is clear — having to nervously see out a 3-1 lead through the final 12 minutes shows the defence still needs to tighten its focus. But as long as there’s a goalscoring machine like Haaland, this game showed City can win any match.
