🔴 LASK 3-6 Liverpool — Barcola Hat-Trick in a 9-Goal Crazy Night

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-14

Result: LASK Linz 3 – 6 Liverpool

Liverpool erased the pain of Sunday’s 0-3 home loss to Man City within an hour at Linz. 19 shots in the first half alone — a Champions League away first-half record — as Liverpool completely dominated. Ronald Araújo’s 22nd-minute header (his first for the club) opened the door, and 2 minutes later Bradley Barcola scored his first. In the 37th Barcola scored his second from Wirtz’s pass and the half ended 0-3. In the 51st Frimpong’s header made it 0-4 and everyone thought the game was done — but the next 30 minutes were the real story of this game.

After Van Dijk and Gomez came on in the 62nd, Liverpool’s defence suddenly collapsed. A minute after coming on, Ljubičić scored (63rd), Barcola completed his hat-trick in the 66th, and Koumassi made it 6-1 in the 72nd — but LASK substitute Flecker scored twice in 3 minutes (74th and 77th) to drag it back to 6-3. Dibago’s 3 assists created all 3 of LASK’s goals. Leading 6-1, Liverpool lost focus and the defence stopped completely — that is the biggest warning for Iraola.

The main man was Barcola. The French winger, who arrived from PSG for £123m and had faced criticism with no goals or assists in 6 games, answered with a hat-trick tonight — the first hat-trick of his career. Wirtz also answered criticism with creativity, and Frimpong was the best player on the pitch with 1 goal + 2 assists. Tsimikas’s 2 assists became 2 goals, and 19-year-old Trey Nyoni also impressed in midfield alongside Gravenberch. Without Isak, Gakpo and Szoboszlai, attacking this much with youngsters and backups showed Liverpool’s squad strength.

With this win Liverpool have 6 points from 2 Champions League games (after the 2-1 win over Atlético on matchday 1). The attack has woken up again and Barcola’s confidence is back — important support for Saturday’s away Premier League game at Brentford and the next home game against Villarreal. But conceding 3 goals in the last 30 minutes was a clear warning for Iraola — even if the game can be finished early, focus must be kept for the full 90. With this kind of defence they’ll pay against bigger opponents.
