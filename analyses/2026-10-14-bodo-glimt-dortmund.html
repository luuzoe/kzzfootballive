🥶 Bodø/Glimt 1-0 Dortmund — A Lone Goal Cools Dortmund Inside the Arctic Circle

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-14

Result: Bodo/Glimt 1 – 0 Borussia Dortmund

A tight game decided by a single goal. In the 52nd minute Fredrik Sjøvold fired home with his left foot from Sondre Auklend’s cross to put Bodø/Glimt ahead. Dortmund threw on 4 subs at once in the 60th (Nmecha, Nwaneri, Guirassy, Ryerson) and added Chukwuemeka in the 77th, but couldn’t break Bodø’s disciplined defence. The hosts calmly managed the game to the end.

The numbers show Dortmund’s frustration — 52.2% possession, 10 corners (Bodø 4), but only 4 of 9 shots on target. No goal from 10 corners showed wasted, murdered chances. Two early yellows (Gadou 13th, Fábio Silva 25th) also kept Dortmund unsettled. On the other side Bodø played with discipline — zero yellows, only 7 fouls, and made the crucial 1 goal count from 3 on target of 11 shots.

For Bodø it was a huge result, bouncing back at their Arctic Circle home after the 5-0 loss to Bayern on matchday 1 — beating big sides at this ground is becoming their trademark. For Dortmund, after the 3-2 win over Villarreal on matchday 1, this away defeat is a step backwards — they need to convert possession and corners into goals and be more effective away from home.
