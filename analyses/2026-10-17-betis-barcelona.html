🔥 Real Betis 2-5 Barcelona — A Red Card That Changed the Game in a Five-Goal Rout

⚔️ La Liga • 2026-10-17

Result: Real Betis 2 – 5 Barcelona

Lamine Yamal opened the scoring in the 10th minute from Koundé’s pass with a strike from outside the box, but Valentín Gómez levelled for Betis in the 27th minute with a header from Fornals’ pass. Just before half-time, in the 41st minute, Rodi restored Barcelona’s lead with a header from Fermín López’s pass. Barcelona enjoyed up to 69.3% possession.

Early in the second half, Gabriel Jesus made it 3-1 in the 51st minute from Yamal’s pass, but two minutes later Abde Ezzalzouli pulled one back to 3-2 from outside the box off Lo Celso’s pass. But the turning point came just a minute later — Facundo Bernal, already on a yellow, was shown a straight red in the 54th minute for a rough challenge, leaving Betis with 10 men. After that Barcelona took full control, with Eric García (from Cubarsí’s pass) in the 62nd minute and Dani Olmo (from Cubarsí’s pass) in the 81st adding further goals.

For Barcelona this was a show of squad depth — two defenders (Rodi, Eric García) scored and Cubarsí created 2 goals. Betis genuinely competed while it was 11 v 11 — managing 5 shots on target. But the red card finished the game. One worry: Jesus had to be replaced by Gavi in the 79th minute through injury — his condition will need watching.
