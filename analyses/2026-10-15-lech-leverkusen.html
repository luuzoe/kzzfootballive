💥 Lech Poznań 0-4 Leverkusen — Schick’s Penalty Double Blows Them Away

⚔️ UEFA Europa League • 2026-10-15

Result: Lech Poznan 0 – 4 Bayer Leverkusen

Leverkusen finished the game in the first half at Lech Poznań’s home ground. Patrik Schick, who came on in the 18th minute for the injured Terrier, converted two penalties in the 29th and 41st minutes into opposite corners to make it 3-0. In between, Malik Tillman had scored the second goal from close range in the 32nd minute. Conceding three goals in 12 first-half minutes was an unrecoverable blow for Poznań.

In the second half Poznań tried to drag the game back with a triple substitution in the 60th minute but nothing changed. The numbers tell the story clearly — Leverkusen controlled the game with 60.9% possession, 22 shots, 10 on target, while Poznań had only 3 on target from 10 shots. Leverkusen committed 16 fouls but didn’t get a red card.

This was a game that showed Leverkusen’s bench strength — a substitute striker like Schick can change the game and a young player like Maza can score in the final minutes. Such an away rout in the Europa League puts Leverkusen in an excellent position for the top of the group.
