🔥 Strasbourg 1-3 PSG — Dembélé’s Second-Half Return Powers a Comeback Win

⚔️ Ligue 1 • 2026-10-17

Result: Strasbourg 1 – 3 Paris Saint-Germain

The first half at Strasbourg’s home ground belonged to the hosts. Giovanni Reyna opened the scoring in the 17th minute from Yacine’s pass with a left-footed strike, putting Strasbourg ahead. PSG had up to 73% possession but couldn’t break through in the first half. The turning point was the second-half substitutions — Nuno Mendes, Doué and Vitinha came on in the 53rd and 54th minutes, followed by Hakimi and Dembélé in the 63rd.

Dembélé missed a penalty in the 72nd minute, but it didn’t stop him. From his corner in the 77th minute, Zabarnyi scored the equaliser — not a header, but a right-footed strike. In the 85th Dembélé scored the go-ahead goal himself with his left foot, and in stoppage time Doué sealed it with the third from Ferran Torres’s pass in the 90+3rd minute. One goal + one assist within 15 minutes of missing a penalty showed Dembélé’s mentality and quality.

A disappointing result for Strasbourg, but their first-half display was noteworthy — with only 27% possession they created 16 shots (8 on target). However, Baldé (60th) and Diop (75th) going off injured hurt their resilience. For PSG this was the mark of a champion side — losing at half-time, missing a penalty, yet turning the game around in the second half with the strength of the bench. In Ligue 1, that ability to come from behind is the most important thing for the title.
