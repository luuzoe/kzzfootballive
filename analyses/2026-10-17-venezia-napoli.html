😱 Venezia 1-0 Napoli — Stanković’s Penalty Save Seals the Bottom Side’s Biggest Upset

⚔️ Serie A • 2026-10-17

Result: Venezia 1 – 0 Napoli

The first half ended goalless but the game was nearly one-sided. Napoli controlled the game with 70.7% possession, 735 passes and 12 corners, but couldn’t break down Venezia’s deep defence. The game changed 4 minutes into the second half — in the 49th minute Akor Adams fired home from the middle of the area with his right foot into the bottom-left corner off Simon Sohm’s quick counter-attack, putting the hosts ahead. Venezia won zero corners all game but made full use of the one chance they got.

The biggest turning point came in the 68th minute. Napoli — who had made a triple substitution in the 54th (Højlund, Elisson, De Bruyne) — won a penalty, but Venezia keeper Filip Stanković saved substitute Kevin De Bruyne’s right-footed effort low to the left corner. Had that penalty gone in, the game could have gone another way. Napoli then brought on Politano (63rd) and Olivera (82nd) but couldn’t break the Venezia defence — the hosts protected their goal with 50 clearances.

The main man was keeper Stanković — 9 saves plus the penalty stop against Napoli’s 29 shots (10 on target). On the other side Napoli had the ball but made nothing of it — 0 goals from 29 shots, 5 offsides, and only 7 accurate of 31 crosses showed how blunt their attack was. Adams decided the game with one goal, and Sohm also shone with the assist. Notably Napoli didn’t pick up a single yellow card — a game lost politely without any aggression.

For Venezia this is a lifesaving win at a time when they’d gone 5 games without a win — it will give them spirit and points to climb out of the drop zone. For Napoli it was an unacceptable dropped points total for a title-chasing side — failing to score against a side near the bottom shows they must urgently fix their attacking efficiency and penalty finishing. On a night even a player like De Bruyne missed a penalty, Napoli don’t have much time left to find answers.
