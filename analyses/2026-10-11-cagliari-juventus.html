🤝 Cagliari 1-1 Juventus — Caprile’s 9 Saves Steal a Point

⚔️ Serie A • 2026-10-11

Result: Cagliari 1 – 1 Juventus

Cagliari played defence-first at home and kept Juventus at bay for over 80 minutes. In the 20th minute Daniel Maldini fired home Paul Mandi’s quick counter-attack pass with his left foot for the opener. Juventus pressed with 71.6% possession and 22 shots, but keeper Elia Caprile’s 9 saves kept them out. Only in the 81st did Kerem Alajbegvić’s cross find Randal Kolo Muani’s head for the equaliser.

Juventus’s problem wasn’t creation but finishing. Of 22 shots, 10 were on target, but Caprile saved Zegrova’s 3 efforts, González’s, Woltemade’s and Cambiaso’s — undoubtedly the man of the match. They tried 35 crosses and finally the equaliser came from Alajbegvić’s cross. Cagliari had only 28.4% possession but made the one counter count for a goal. 3 yellows came out (McKennie 17th, Rodríguez 45+1st, Diola 83rd).

For Juventus this 1 draw point was disappointing. For a title-chasing side, 1 goal from 22 shots showed an attacking efficiency problem — against more organised defensive sides with in-form keepers, they could drop more points in coming games. For Cagliari the home point was valuable — Caprile’s form and disciplined defensive organisation are a good foundation for a good season.
