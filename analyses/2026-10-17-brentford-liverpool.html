😤 Brentford 1-2 Liverpool — Liverpool Come From Behind Against 10-Man Brentford

⚔️ Premier League • 2026-10-17

Result: Brentford 1 – 2 Liverpool

The start offered chances for Liverpool — in the 20th minute Barcola missed a one-on-one with the goalkeeper, and Mac Allister’s penalty appeal was waved off after a VAR check. In the 44th the hosts took the lead — Lewis-Potter headed home Thiago’s cross. The game changed in first-half stoppage time — Yarmoliuk was shown a red card after a VAR check for a headbutt on Jakae. In the second half Liverpool used the advantage — Muñoz equalised in the 62nd from Wirtz’s pass, and Isak scored the winner in the 70th from Barcola’s headed assist. In stoppage time Janelt picked up a second yellow, and Brentford finished the game with 9 men.

The main man was Muñoz — he troubled the Brentford defence all game, scored the equaliser, and shifted to right-back after Frimpong went off injured. Isak was barely in the game until the 69th minute, but with one chance he finished the winner precisely — his second straight match-winning goal. On the other side Barcola had a mixed day — he missed 2 goalscoring chances in the first half, but set up Isak in the second and hit the post once. Injuries also hurt Liverpool — Szoboszlai went off in the 32nd minute (Nyoni on) and Frimpong lasted only 7 minutes of the second half (Ngumoha on). The numbers show Liverpool’s dominance — 59.9% possession, 21 shots (Brentford 10), 5 on target (Brentford 2), 9 corners (Brentford 3).

For Brentford this defeat ends their unbeaten start to the season — they defended with 10 men for a whole half but couldn’t hold the win. Still, their spirit and organisation deserve praise. For Liverpool it was a bounce-back from the 3-0 loss to Man City 6 days ago — including the 6-3 Champions League win over LASK in between. But this wasn’t a win that inspires full confidence — against opponents down to 10 men (then 9) they managed only 5 shots on target, and the injury list is growing. How long Szoboszlai and Frimpong will be out is now the big question for the coming games.
