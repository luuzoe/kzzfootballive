😱 Inter 3-4 Club Brugge — Tresoldi’s Hat-Trick Delivers the San Siro Shock

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-13

Result: Internazionale 3 – 4 Club Brugge

A 7-goal crazy night at San Siro. Club Brugge shocked Inter with Nicolò Tresoldi’s hat-trick. In the 30th minute Çalhanoğlu’s tackle on Carlos Forbes gave away a penalty and Tresoldi opened the scoring. Lautaro Martínez equalised in the 44th, but in the second half Tresoldi scored in the 63rd (from Forbes’s pass) and 74th to complete the hat-trick. Lautaro gave hope back with his second in the 81st, but substitute Romeo Vermant scored the fourth in the 87th. Ange-Yoan Bonny’s 90+3rd-minute goal was only a consolation.

Inter’s defensive mistakes were costly in this game — the tackle that gave away the penalty, and the second goal where Forbes slotted the ball through keeper Josep Martínez’s legs from a defensive misunderstanding, teeing up an easy finish for Tresoldi. Inter had 30 shots (12 on target) but only got 3 goals — the Brugge keeper’s 9 saves frustrated Inter. On the other side Brugge’s efficiency was amazing — 4 goals from 8 on target. Carlos Forbes, who won the penalty and made 2 assists, could be called the man of the match.

Such a home defeat is a big blow to Inter’s Champions League journey — they need to find defensive stability again. For Club Brugge it was the night they announced their name in Europe — scoring 4 at San Siro against Inter is an unforgettable result. Tresoldi’s name has spread across Europe.
