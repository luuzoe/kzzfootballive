🔵 Galatasaray 1-4 Barcelona — Yamal’s 2 Assists and the Second-Half Storm

⚔️ UEFA Champions League • 2026-10-13

Result: Galatasaray 1 – 4 Barcelona

In Istanbul Barcelona faced a serious threat. Galatasaray led in the 28th through Oleksii Batrakov’s goal (from Rafael Leão’s assist), making the fans dream. But just before half-time, in the 43rd, João Cancelo equalised from Dani Olmo’s pass — that goal was the turning point. In the second half Barcelona were a storm — Fermín López 50th (from Yamal, header), Anthony Gordon 53rd (from Yamal), substitute Karim Adeyemi 65th.

Lamine Yamal was the brightest star — 2 assists in 3 minutes. Barcelona suffocated Galatasaray with 71% possession and 22 shots (11 on target). Flick’s substitution was also effective — Adeyemi scored 6 minutes after coming on. Galatasaray were caught offside 8 times — trying to get past Barcelona’s high line, but faded in the second half.

With this win Barcelona kept their strong early-season winning form. The bench scoring (Fermín, Adeyemi) is the best news for a long trophy campaign — in a congested season such depth is worth more than gold. Galatasaray showed they can threaten big sides, but need to hold it for the full 90.
